Frequently asked questions

Archival knowledge to help you understand our curation protocols.

How do you ensure medical-grade safety?

We use hospital-grade sterilization, single-use surgical tools, and implant-grade titanium or solid 18k gold exclusively.

What is the 'Anatomy Mapping' process?

It is a technical consultation where we analyze your bone structure, cartilage depth, and blood flow to determine the safest and most aesthetic placement.

Can I book online?

Yes, our private booking portal allows you to select your preferred artist and session type with real-time availability.

Do you offer walk-in appointments?

To maintain our private studio environment and hygiene standards, we operate strictly on a pre-booked appointment basis.

What is your jewelry warranty?

All solid gold and titanium pieces come with a lifetime guarantee against manufacturing defects, ensuring your investment is protected.

How long is the healing process?

Healing varies by placement, typically ranging from 3 to 12 months. Our concierge support guides you through every stage of the maturation.
